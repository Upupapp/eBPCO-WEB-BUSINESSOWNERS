# Purpose

The AI Governance document establishes the policies, responsibilities, decision-making processes, and oversight mechanisms governing the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) throughout the Electronic Business Permit and Clearance Office (eBPCO) project.

The objective is to ensure that AI is used responsibly, ethically, securely, and consistently while maintaining full human accountability for all project deliverables.

These governance standards apply to every phase of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

---

# Objectives

AI Governance aims to:

- Promote responsible AI usage.
- Ensure compliance with project standards.
- Protect sensitive government information.
- Maintain software quality.
- Preserve human accountability.
- Encourage transparency.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Reduce operational and security risks.

---

# Governance Principles

## Human Responsibility

AI is a development assistant, not an autonomous decision-maker.

All technical, architectural, security, and business decisions shall remain under the authority of designated project personnel.

Humans retain full responsibility for every deliverable produced with AI assistance.

---

## Transparency

The use of AI should be transparent throughout the project.

Teams should disclose AI assistance when appropriate for:

- Code generation
- Documentation
- UI design
- Architecture proposals
- Test generation
- Refactoring

Transparency improves traceability and accountability.

---

## Accountability

Every AI-generated artifact shall have an assigned human owner responsible for:

- Verification
- Approval
- Maintenance
- Future modifications

Ownership shall never be delegated to AI systems.

---

## Consistency

AI shall be used in accordance with:

- Project Standards
- Development Guidelines
- Security Policies
- Documentation Standards
- Government regulations
- Organizational policies

Consistent application of standards ensures predictable project outcomes.

---

# Scope of AI Usage

Approved AI-assisted activities include:

- Source code generation
- Documentation drafting
- UI prototyping
- Architecture recommendations
- Test generation
- Code review assistance
- Refactoring assistance
- Bug analysis
- Productivity improvements

Final implementation decisions remain subject to human approval.

---

# Prohibited Uses

AI shall not be used to:

- Approve production deployments.
- Replace human code reviews.
- Make legal or regulatory decisions.
- Approve business permits.
- Access confidential production data without authorization.
- Generate or expose sensitive credentials.
- Circumvent organizational security policies.

Unauthorized AI usage is prohibited.

# Review Process

Every AI-generated artifact shall follow the established review process:

1. AI generates the artifact.
2. Author validates the output.
3. Peer review is completed.
4. Required revisions are made.
5. Testing is performed where applicable.
6. Final approval is granted.
7. Artifact is integrated into the project.

No AI-generated work shall bypass required review procedures.

---

# Risk Management

Potential AI-related risks include:

- Incorrect implementations
- Security vulnerabilities
- Hallucinated information
- Outdated recommendations
- Inconsistent documentation
- Licensing concerns
- Privacy violations

Risks shall be identified, documented, and mitigated before deployment.

---

# Compliance

AI-assisted development shall comply with:

- Internal project standards
- Organizational policies
- Government regulations
- Security requirements
- Accessibility standards
- Data privacy laws

Compliance is mandatory for all project contributors.

---

# Audit and Traceability

AI-assisted activities should be traceable.

Project records should include:

- AI-generated artifacts
- Review history
- Approval records
- Significant architectural decisions
- Major revisions

Traceability supports accountability and future maintenance.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Improvements may include:

- Updated prompting techniques
- New development workflows
- Enhanced security controls
- Revised documentation standards
- Lessons learned from project experience

Governance shall evolve alongside AI capabilities and project needs.
