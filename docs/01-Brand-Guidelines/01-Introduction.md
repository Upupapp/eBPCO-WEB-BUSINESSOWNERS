# Purpose

The Electronic Business Permit and Clearance Office (eBPCO) Design System provides a unified visual language and user experience framework for all eBPCO digital products.

This document establishes the design foundation that governs the appearance, behavior, and consistency of every interface across the eBPCO ecosystem. It ensures that all users receive a professional, intuitive, accessible, and consistent experience regardless of the platform they are using.

The Brand Guidelines serve as the official reference for designers, frontend developers, quality assurance engineers, project managers, and stakeholders involved in the development and maintenance of eBPCO.

---

# About eBPCO

Electronic Business Permit and Clearance Office (eBPCO) is a digital platform designed to modernize and simplify the process of applying for, reviewing, approving, and managing business permits within Local Government Units (LGUs).

The system replaces fragmented manual processes with a centralized, transparent, and user-friendly digital experience for both government employees and business owners.

The platform consists of two primary applications:

## Web Administration Portal

The Angular-based administration portal is used by government personnel to manage applications, monitor workflows, evaluate requirements, issue approvals, and administer the overall system.

Primary users include:

- Super Administrator
- BPLO Staff
- Treasurer
- Zoning Officer
- Fire Safety Officer
- Health Officer
- Engineering Officer
- Mayor's Office
- Other authorized government personnel

---

## Mobile Application

The Flutter-based mobile application is intended for business owners and applicants.

Users may:

- Register an account
- Submit permit applications
- Track application status
- Receive notifications
- Upload documentary requirements
- Monitor payment status
- View digital receipts
- Receive permit updates

To become the standard digital platform that enables efficient, transparent, and citizen-centered business permit processing for Local Government Units throughout the Philippines.

---

# Mission

To provide government agencies and citizens with a secure, accessible, and easy-to-use digital platform that simplifies business permit processing while improving operational efficiency and public service delivery.

# Objectives

The eBPCO Design System aims to:

- Establish a consistent visual identity.
- Improve usability across all platforms.
- Increase accessibility for all users.
- Reduce design inconsistencies.
- Improve development efficiency through reusable components.
- Maintain professional government branding.
- Support long-term scalability and maintainability.

# Design Philosophy

Every interface within eBPCO should follow the following principles.

## Simplicity

Users should accomplish their tasks with as few steps as possible.

---

## Consistency

Every component should behave predictably across all screens and platforms.

---

## Accessibility

Interfaces must remain usable for users with varying abilities and technical experience.

---

## Transparency

The system should clearly communicate application status, progress, errors, approvals, and required actions.

---

## Efficiency

Administrative users should complete repetitive tasks quickly using optimized workflows.

---

## Trust

The interface should reinforce professionalism through clean layouts, appropriate spacing, consistent typography, and government-standard visual design.

---

# Design Governance

The Brand Guidelines are the official source of truth for all design-related decisions.

No new interface, component, pattern, or interaction may be introduced unless it is documented and approved within the Design System.

Whenever changes are made:

1. Documentation must be updated first.
2. Design review must be completed.
3. Components must be updated.
4. Implementation may then begin.

This process ensures long-term consistency across both the Angular Web Administration Portal and the Flutter Mobile Application.
